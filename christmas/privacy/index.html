Christmas Song-o-ganza App and Site Privacy PolicyEffective Date: November 18, 2026

The short version: your game stays on your device. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking, and we don't sell or share your information. The App sends us only one thing: a technical crash report when something goes wrong. Anything else leaves your device only when you choose to share your title image or save a backup somewhere.

1.

Privacy Policy Overview

Christmas Song-o-ganza is a free Christmas song listening game published by Cupbearer, LLC, doing business as Middlebrook.Dev (“Middlebrook.Dev”, “we”, “us”, “our”). This Privacy Policy explains what information the Christmas Song-o-ganza mobile application (the “App”) and this website (the “Site”) collect, use, share, retain, and protect. It applies to all users of and visitors to the App and Site (“you”, “your”, or “user”).

This Privacy Policy is incorporated by reference into our Terms of Use. We designed the App to work without collecting personal information, and this policy describes how.

2.

Information We Collect

“Personal information” in this policy means information that can reasonably be linked to an identifiable individual. We do not ask for, and the App does not collect, your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any payment information.

Crash and error reports. This is the only information the App sends to us. When the App crashes or encounters an error, it automatically sends a technical report to Sentry, a crash reporting service operated by Functional Software, Inc. A report may include:

  • technical details about the error, such as what went wrong and where in the App's code;
  • your device model, operating system and version, App version, and similar technical information about the device and App at the time of the error; and
  • a short record of recent activity in the App leading up to the error, such as which screens were open and which buttons were tapped.

Crash reports are not linked to your identity. They are not designed to include your name or anything you type into the App, and we have configured the App not to send IP addresses or other personal data to Sentry. Crash reports are not used for advertising or tracking.

Site. The Site does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking technologies. Like any website, the service that hosts the Site may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, for security and operations.

Email. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message.

3.

Information That Stays on Your Device

Your game data, including your progress, the songs you've marked, the name you enter, your titles, and your settings, is stored only on your device. We never receive it. Deleting the App deletes this information.

Backup files. If you make a backup, the App creates a file containing only your game data and saves it wherever you choose, such as your Files app or a cloud drive you use. If you restore from a backup, the App reads only the file you pick. We never receive your backups. Backup files are encrypted so they can't easily be edited; anyone who has the file and the App can still restore it, so keep it somewhere you trust.

Sharing your title image. When you tap Share, the App creates an image of your title and hands it to your device's share menu. It goes only to the app, service, or person you pick, and from there is subject to their privacy practices. We don't receive it. On iPhone, the App asks for permission to add photos only so you can save your title image to your Photos; the App never reads or accesses your existing photos.

4.

How We Use Information

We use crash and error reports only to find, diagnose, and fix problems in the App and to keep it working properly. We use emails you send us only to respond to you. We may also use information as necessary to comply with a legal obligation or to protect our rights, our users, and the App.

5.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

  • Service providers. Crash reports are processed and stored by Sentry on our behalf, for the purpose of helping us fix the App. Sentry's handling of this data is governed by its agreement with us and its privacy policy.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required by law, legal process, or a government request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of us, our users, or others.
  • Business transfers. If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction and would remain subject to this Privacy Policy.
6.

How We Protect Information

We follow privacy-by-design principles: the best protection is not collecting data in the first place, so the App keeps your game on your device and sends only minimal technical crash data. Crash reports are sent over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Email is not a secure channel, so please don't email us sensitive information.

Retention. Sentry keeps crash reports for a limited period, after which they are deleted automatically. We keep emails only as long as needed to respond and for our reasonable records.

7.

Links to Other Websites

The App and Site link to other websites, such as the Christmas Song-o-ganza game website and a Facebook group. Those websites are independent of us and are not governed by this Privacy Policy. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, and we encourage you to read their privacy policies when you visit them.

8.

Your Choices and Rights

Because your game data never leaves your device, you are in control of it: you can view it in the App, back it up, or delete it by deleting the App.

Depending on where you live, including California, other U.S. states, the European Economic Area, and the United Kingdom, you may have the right to: (1) be informed about how we collect, use, and disclose personal information; (2) know whether we hold personal information about you and get a copy; (3) correct it; (4) ask us to delete or restrict it; (5) object to our processing of it; (6) file a complaint with your local data protection regulator; and (7) not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. Because crash reports aren't linked to your identity, we may not be able to find reports related to you, but we will do what we reasonably can. To make a request, email us at the address below.

Shine the Light. We do not share personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Global Privacy Control. The Site doesn't track you, so there is nothing for a Global Privacy Control signal to opt you out of. We honor these signals anyway.

9.

No Marketing Communications

We don't collect email addresses through the App and don't send marketing. If you email us, we will use your address only to reply.

10.

International Transfer

We are based in the United States, and crash reports are processed and stored by Sentry in the United States. If you use the App outside the United States, crash reports from your device will be transferred to and processed in the United States.

11.

Lawful Bases for Processing

Where the law requires a legal basis for processing personal information, we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the App stable, secure, and working properly (for crash reports), on performing our agreement with you or responding to your request (for emails you send us), and on complying with legal obligations where applicable.

12.

If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under thirteen, we will promptly delete it unless we are legally required to keep it.
